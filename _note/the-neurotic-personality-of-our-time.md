There are two characteristics, however, which one may discern in all neuroses without having an intimate knowledge of the personality structure: a certain rigidity in reaction and a discrepancy between potentialities and accomplishments. (P22)

然而，不需要熟悉地了解人格结构，人们也可以从所有神经症患者身上辨别出两种特征：反应的僵化，以及潜能与实际成就之间的差距。
